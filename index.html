Your image never leaves this device. Everything runs in this page as plain JavaScript: nothing is uploaded and there is no server.

The outline is drawn by a JavaScript port of segment.py (mean Dice 0.842 on 800 held-out ultrasound frames), and the measurements by a port of features.py, both checked against the original Python on the project's own images. One L2-regularised logistic model per descriptor, fitted on the training split only, turns them into the probabilities above; fixed ACR rules turn those into points and a TR level. No neural network, no learned image filters, no stored image data.

Reliability chips show each descriptor's ROC AUC on the 753-image validation split, so you can see which parts of the score to trust. Margin descriptors are the weakest — ill-defined margin reaches only 0.70 — and punctate foci are hard to see in small crops.
